52 Solo Leveling: Arise from the Shadow Completed 8/10 TV 13/13 The pacing is much worse in season 2 than season 1 but it's made up for by the amazing fights and the fact that I just enjoyed the necromancer arc more than the assassin arc. The first 2 or so episodes were when this peaked. Igris is actually my fucking goat, he's easily my favourite character so far with his dedication to his master and the way that he fights, it's all incredibly cool. I think that's the one thing that I can say about Solo Leveling; it's just really cool. I also did some research and found out that the reason so many people hated it was because there "weren't enough stakes, it was boring just watching Sung win every fight" and that really surprised me. The funny thing is, most of these takes were also coming from people who have watched and really like other shonen/seinen series. That's not the point that Solo Leveling is going for. It's a power fantasy in its purest form. There aren't many important side characters, the plot is pretty straight forward, and the appeal isn't wondering whether Sung will lose, the appeal is watching him get stronger, unlock new abilities, and destroy everything in front of him. Just like a god build in an MMO, which is this anime's direct inspiration. If you go into it with the expectation of a high stakes shonen where important characters can die at any moment, that makes sense why you would be disappointed, but that misses the point. The point of Solo Leveling is watching Sung get stronger and be really cool and kill the bad guy, that's it. Not every anime needs high stakes or constant uncertainty, sometimes the enjoyment can just be watching an overpowered protag dominate strong opponents and get better at doing so. It's just a really simple anime, and a pretty good one at that. Anyway, yeah, I really liked it, it was cool. My takes on voice acting, audio, music, and animation all remain from my review on season 1. Also the OP being done by LiSA and the ED being done by Ling tosite sigure were kinda cool. Bug man at the end was also really cool, the whole ending was really good imo.
